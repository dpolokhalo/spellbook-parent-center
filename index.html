FOR GROWN-UPS

Outdoor quests with the grown-up in control.

Spellbook turns a playground, park, or backyard visit into short movement, making, noticing, and storytelling quests.

Read the privacy policy Get support
◉

Grown-up supervised

The grown-up keeps the iPhone and sets a safe play boundary. The app supports supervision; it never replaces it.

⌁

Works offline

Quests, read-aloud prompts, camera evidence, compass activities, and progress work without an account or network connection.

◇

No ads or tracking

No advertising, analytics, social feed, child profile, behavioral tracking, or third-party SDK is included.

Camera evidence is not a perfect judge

The camera can look for broad objects, connected triangle sides, and a steady body pose. It cannot understand a child’s complete construction, story, or learning. Camera quests require a grown-up’s final confirmation.
